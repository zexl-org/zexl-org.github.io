The week

Both American inflation prints came in soft. Japan's came in near a three-year high.

The same barrel of oil did both. Washington got two soft prints in two days — consumer prices at 3.4%, producer prices at 4.7%, each below what forecasters expected. Tokyo reported that its firms are paying 7.2% more than a year ago. The committee that has to decide in September ended the week exactly as split as it began it.

Two tenths below consensus, and the mechanism was the same one as June: energy. Final demand goods fell 0.7% on a 3.1% drop in energy and a 5.7% slide in gasoline, while services rose 0.2% and construction jumped 2.2%. Crude petroleum in the intermediate stages fell 11.9%. The oil that has been driving every question on this board pushed producer prices down this month, not up.
